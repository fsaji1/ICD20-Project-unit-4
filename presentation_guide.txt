SELF-DRIVING CARS WEBSITE - PRESENTATION GUIDE

OPENING:
- Welcome audience and introduce autonomous vehicles as the future of transportation
- This covers what they are, problems they solve, how they work, and real applications
- Mention this technology is already being tested and used today

WHAT THEY ARE:
- Define AVs: vehicles that operate safely with little to no human input
- Key technologies: LiDAR sensors, cameras, radar, AI, GPS mapping, edge computing
- Explain the 5 automation levels from Level 0 (no automation) to Level 5 (full automation)
- We're currently at Level 2-4 with companies actively developing Level 5

PROBLEMS ADDRESSED:
- Road Safety: 94% of accidents are human error - AVs eliminate this
- Traffic Congestion: Optimized driving patterns and better coordination
- Accessibility: Independence for disabled, elderly, and those without licenses
- Environmental Impact: Electric vehicles with optimized routes reduce emissions

HOW THEY WORK:
- 5-step cycle that repeats hundreds of times per second:
  1. Perception (sensors gather data)
  2. Localization (GPS determines location)
  3. Prediction (AI predicts other road users' behavior)
  4. Planning (decides safest route and actions)
  5. Control (executes steering, acceleration, braking)
- Emphasize the speed allows faster reactions than humans

REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS:
- Ride-Sharing & Taxis: Waymo and Cruise operating autonomous taxi services
- Freight & Delivery: Self-driving trucks for long hauls, cost reduction
- Last-Mile Delivery: Autonomous robots for package delivery
- Public Transit: Autonomous buses for efficiency and reduced costs

CLOSING:
- Summarize the transformative impact of autonomous vehicles
- Mention this is already happening, not just future technology
- Note challenges: regulation, cybersecurity, public acceptance
- Entire presentation is in ONE HTML file - easy to share and modify
- Open for questions and discussion
